What to have ready when you call

A minute of preparation makes the call faster.

  • Your street address.

    Include the nearest cross street. Wildwood ZIP codes and town names overlap, so the address matters more than the ZIP.

  • What you see.

    Where the water is, whether it is clear or dirty, and whether it is moving or standing still.

  • Whether the main valve is shut.

    If you could not shut it, say so. That changes the urgency.

  • Water source.

    City water from Missouri American Water or a private well. It tells the plumber which pipes you own.

  • Age and type of the equipment.

    For a water heater, say gas or electric, tank or tankless, and roughly how old. A photo of the label on the tank helps when the plumber arrives.

  • A callback number.

    If the call drops, the dispatcher needs a way to reach you.

If it is an emergency, do this before calling

  1. 1

    Shut off the main water valve.

    Turn it clockwise. It is usually where the water line enters the house, often in the basement or a utility room.

  2. 2

    Stop using water and drains.

    No flushing, no washer, no dishwasher while sewage or water is rising.

  3. 3

    Stay out of wet electrical areas.

    Do not touch wet outlets or stand in water near a panel.

  4. 4

    Document what you see.

    Take photos before cleanup for your insurer or landlord. This is the moment to mention water damage so the plumber knows how far it spread.

If you smell gas, leave first

Leave the building immediately and go somewhere you cannot smell gas. Once you are safe, call Spire's 24/7 line at 800-887-4173 or 911, then call a plumber. Do not use lights, garage door openers or alarm systems on the way out. Stay out until a gas safety professional says it is safe. Plumbers do not handle gas leaks, and we will not dispatch to one.

Which service do you need?

If you are unsure, start with the symptom. Water pouring from a pipe points toward burst pipe repair, a backing up toilet points toward drain cleaning, and a leaking tank points toward water heater repair. Our services page lists every service by symptom.
