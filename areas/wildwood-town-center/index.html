Plumber in Wildwood Town Center, MO

Wildwood Town Center is the city's planned hub for shops, civic buildings, a college campus and higher-density housing around the Highway 100 and Highway 109 intersection. It is a commercial and mixed-use district, not a single subdivision, and this page is written that way.

What the Town Center is

The city's Master Plan centers the Town Center on the Highway 109 and Highway 100 intersection. The city chose it as its hub for higher-density development because of its topography, utility access, existing infrastructure and historical significance.

The Wildwood Municipal Building is at 16860 Main Street, beside the six-acre Village Green civic space. St. Louis Community College Wildwood also has a building of more than 130,000 square feet here.

For a plumber, this means shared lines and many users, so the first question is usually which building and which units share the problem.

Housing planned or reported nearby

A May 2025 West Newsmagazine report described a Greenberg Development mixed-use building with 188 multifamily units and ground-floor commercial space at Main Street and Taylor Road. It also described The Reserve at Wildwood, with 115 detached homes on 50 acres, and The Pointe at Brightleaf, with 30 detached homes.

That report described most projects as planned, approved or under construction. We have not confirmed that any building is complete, so check current status before relying on a project's details.

If your home or unit is new, ask about the builder's warranty before you pay for a repair.

Sewer and water: mostly public, not entirely

The Master Plan describes most of the Town Center area as inside public sewer and water service areas that still rely in part on individual systems. City code section 510.080 requires connection to a public sewer when a sanitary line is within 200 feet.

For an owner or manager, confirm early whether the building is on the public system, a private line or its own system. That decides who owns the line. A sewer camera inspection shows the actual condition.
