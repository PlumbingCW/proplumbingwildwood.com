What to check on a water heater before you call

This is general guidance, not a claim about homes in Pond, where we have no verified housing data.

  • Read the age label.

    The rating plate shows the manufacture date and helps decide between repair and replacement.

  • Look at the base of the tank.

    Moisture at the base often points to a leak in the tank itself, which usually means replacement.

  • Check the shutoff valves.

    Shut the cold supply if water is escaping, and turn off power or gas to the heater if you can do it safely.

  • Note the symptoms.

    Tell us whether you have no hot water, lukewarm water, noise or a drip.
