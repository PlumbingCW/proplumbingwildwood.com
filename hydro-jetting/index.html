Emergency Hydro Jetting in Wildwood, MO

Hydro jetting in Wildwood, MO is a drain-clearing method that sends high-pressure water through a hose and nozzle to strip roots, grease and scale from the inside of a pipe. A cable pokes a hole through a clog, and jetting cleans the whole pipe wall.

If a main drain is backing up into a tub or floor drain, our 24/7 emergency line is open. Stop using water in the house, keep the washer off, and leave the cleanout capped until we arrive.

Why does my main drain keep clogging every couple of years?

A drain that clogs, gets cabled, and clogs again two or three years later is usually coated, not just plugged. Grease, soap scum, mineral film and fine roots narrow the pipe a little each month. The cable opens a channel through the middle and leaves the wall layer behind, so the channel closes again.

Hydro jetting is the answer to that pattern because water pressure scours the full circle of the pipe. When roots are the cause, a jetting nozzle clears the root mass from the joint and removes the fine threads a cable tends to skip. That is why homeowners who have paid for repeat cabling often ask for jetting next.

Why roots and repeat clogs are common in Wildwood

The city's Master Plan describes Wildwood as rolling and hilly woodlands of the Ozark foothills, and most lots keep trees near the house. Roots go where the moisture is, and a sewer lateral joint is a steady source of it.

Census ACS data for 2020 to 2024 puts the median Wildwood home at a 1992 build year, with about 42.8 percent of homes built before 1990. Those lots have had 30 years or more of tree growth, which is long enough for roots to reach a joint and settle in.

Older areas around Grover and Pond along Old Manchester Road add another unknown: we only call a pipe material after a camera has looked at it. A jetting visit usually includes a camera pass so you see the result.

How a jetting visit works

  1. 1

    Find the access point.

    We locate a cleanout, or pull a toilet when none exists, so the hose enters the line close to the problem.

  2. 2

    Feed the nozzle in.

    The hose advances with the nozzle sending water forward and back. Rear jets pull the hose along the pipe as the water works.

  3. 3

    Pull back with full cleaning.

    The real scouring happens on the return, when the water strips the wall and flushes debris toward the main or the tank.

  4. 4

    Run a camera and flush the fixtures.

    A pass with the inspection camera confirms the pipe is clear and shows what remains, such as a belly or a cracked joint.

When jetting is the wrong tool

Jetting is aggressive, so it is the wrong tool for some pipes. A cracked, collapsed or badly offset line can wash out soil around the break when high-pressure water runs through it. Brittle old cast iron with heavy scale may shed in chunks that need to be collected, not flushed. If a camera shows damage, the answer is a repair, and we explain the options on our sewer line repair page.

A blockage that is a foreign object, such as a toy or a broken cable end, may need to be pulled by hand rather than pushed along. And a drain that is slow because of a vent problem or a full septic tank will not improve with water pressure at all.

Jetting a home on septic or a package plant

Many Wildwood homes, especially west of Highway 109, are not on public sewer, and the city's Master Plan notes that soils in much of the city are unsuitable for individual septic systems. On a private system we jet the line from the house to the tank and stop there, because pushing a lot of water into the tank and field can stir solids and overload them.

Skip caustic drain products on any septic or shared plant. They can harm the bacteria that digest waste, and the dose passes into a field or plant not built for it.

Jetting for landlords, restaurants and busy households

A line that serves several bathrooms, a rental or a commercial kitchen builds coating faster than a single-family drain. Scheduled jetting every year or two can cost less than one weekend backup and the cleanup that follows. For restaurants and shops at places like Wildwood Town Center, our commercial plumbing team handles grease and drain schedules.

If jetting does not hold, a basic drain cleaning visit with a cable may be enough for the branch lines, while the main gets the pressure treatment. We help you pick the cheaper split.
