Tankless Water Heater in Wildwood, MO

A tankless water heater in Wildwood, MO is an on-demand unit that heats water as it flows through the heat exchanger instead of storing it in a tank. Why does my hot water run cold or run out?

People usually reach for a tankless unit after one of three frustrations: a tank that runs out mid-shower, a heater taking up floor space, or a tank that has just failed. A tank holds a fixed volume, so a long shower plus a dishwasher can drain it. A tankless unit has no tank to run dry, but it has a limit of its own: the flow rate it can heat at once.

Each model is rated for a temperature rise at a given flow. In winter, incoming water is colder, so the same unit delivers fewer gallons per minute at the same output temperature. That is why sizing is done against your coldest inlet water and your busiest hour, not an average day.

Gas supply and venting in plain words

A gas tankless heater burns a lot of fuel in a short burst, so the gas line feeding it must carry more volume than a tank heater needs. Often that means a larger pipe from the meter or a branch, and sometimes a regulator change. We size the gas line from the unit's rated input and the length of the run.

The exhaust is the other half. Most tankless units use sealed, fan-driven vents, many in plastic or stainless depending on the model, and condensing types need a drain for acidic condensate. The manufacturer's manual dictates vent material, length and clearance. If you smell gas at any point, leave the building and call Spire at 800-887-4173 or 911 before calling a plumber.

Flush and descale: what hard water does to a tankless unit

Scale is the leading cause of service calls on tankless heaters. As water heats inside the exchanger, dissolved calcium and magnesium come out of solution and coat the inner surfaces. The coating insulates the metal, reduces flow, triggers error codes and shortens the life of the unit.

Missouri American Water supplies Wildwood. Its 2023 report lists total hardness of 66 to 150 ppm at Meramec plants and 82 to 226 ppm at Missouri River plants. The 2025 report does not publish a hardness row, but its calcium and magnesium work out to about 119 mg/L, our calculation. Homes on private wells must test their water, since well hardness is unknown without a lab result.

A descale flush circulates a mild acid, usually white vinegar or a manufacturer-approved solution, through the exchanger using a small pump and the unit's isolation valves. Manufacturers generally call for it at intervals set by water hardness, so follow the manual for yours. Hard water or a well makes more frequent flushing sensible.

Signs your tankless heater needs a flush

These symptoms are common when scale has built up, though other faults can look similar.

  • Error codes.

    Many models flag restricted flow or overheating. The manual's code table points to scale, a clogged inlet filter or a failed sensor.

  • Lower flow.

    Hot water arrives, but less of it. Clogged inlet screens or scaling in the exchanger are common causes.

  • Temperature swings.

    The temperature wanders between hot and warm. This can be scale or a sensor problem.

  • Noise.

    Popping or knocking in the unit can signal mineral buildup on the exchanger.

  • No isolation valves.

    If your unit has none, a flush needs valves added first. We install them as part of the service.

Honest drawbacks

Tankless units cost more up front and often need gas, venting or electrical upgrades. Some give a short burst of cold water between uses, and very low flows may not trigger the burner. Many homeowners find that hot water arrives a little later, because water in the pipes between the unit and the fixture has to be flushed out first.

Electric tankless units pull a very large load, so they may require a panel upgrade. If your hot-water use is low or your gas line is hard to upgrade, a good tank or a heat pump model may serve you better, and we say so.

Installation and repair: how we handle each

For a new tankless install we walk the house, measure gas pipe, plan the vent route and check electrical supply. Sizing considers the number of bathrooms and the coldest inlet water. The planning side overlaps with our water heater installation work, including code and permit questions.

For repair, we read error codes, inspect the inlet filter, test flow and temperature sensors, check gas pressure and venting, and flush if scale is the cause. Permit rules vary, so ask the Wildwood building department before replacing or installing any heater.

What affects the price of a tankless water heater

Prices are not published here. Gas line, venting and unit selection change the total, and you get a clear price before work starts.

Unit capacity.

Bigger units that serve more bathrooms or larger households cost more.

Gas line upgrade.

A larger gas pipe adds materials and labor, depending on the distance from the meter.

Venting.

Longer or more complex vent runs and condensate drains add to the job.

Electrical or panel work.

Electric tankless models may need heavy wiring and breaker capacity.

Water treatment.

Adding a softener or scale filter to protect the exchanger is a separate cost.

Tankless installation and descaling are normally owner expenses. A sudden leak that damages the house may fall under your policy, while scale or wear generally does not. Your policy decides.
