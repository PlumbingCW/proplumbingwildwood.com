What these guides cover

Our posts answer one question at a time. Typical topics include when a water heater is worth fixing, why a sewer line backs up in a hilly subdivision and what a camera inspection can see. Each guide names what a homeowner can safely do and when the job belongs to a licensed plumber.

We write from sources we can name: the St. Louis County Plumbing Code as adopted by the City of Wildwood, utility water quality reports and Census housing data. When we cannot confirm a fact, such as the hardness at your tap, we say to test it and ask your water utility.

Where the guides point you

Most guides end at one of our service pages, such as drain cleaning, water heater replacement or leak detection. The town-specific ones link to Ellisville, Ballwin, Chesterfield and Eureka.

You can also browse the full services list or the areas we serve. We do not list prices in guides unless a named primary source supports the figure.

How we write

Guides are credited to the Wildwood Pro Plumbing team, not to a named person, because the plumbing is done by licensed contractors we dispatch. Where a guide mentions insurance, we say what is usually true and that your policy decides. Where it mentions permits, we say to ask the building department, because rules vary by job.
